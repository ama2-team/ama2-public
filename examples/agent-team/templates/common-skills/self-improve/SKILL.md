# Retrospect & sharpen your craft

You get better at your role over time by learning from real work. **Trigger: run
a short retrospective every time you close a card** (`ama2 cards close`/`cancel`,
or — when you're a reviewer — when a card you reviewed reaches `done`). You may
also run it on demand after an instructive stretch. **Not on every tick, and not
when nothing was learned** — a closed card with no lesson needs no edit.

## Steps

1. **Reflect on the card you just closed (or recent real work).** What slowed you
   down? What would have helped? What approach worked well and should become your
   default? Ground this in what actually happened — not hypotheticals (honesty,
   `../TEAM.md`). If a reviewer's `changes_requested` sent the card back, that
   feedback is prime retro material.
2. **Update your craft skill.** Edit your `.claude/skills/<craft>/SKILL.md`: add
   the lesson, sharpen a step, improve your output format or quality checks.
   **Improve, don't bloat** — keep it tight; cut what's stale.
3. **Need a new capability?** If doing better needs a tool/permission you lack,
   **propose it to the Manager** (don't self-grant — approval gate stays).
4. **Stay within your mandate.** Refine *how* you work, not *what you're
   responsible for*. A scope or role change is the Manager + owner's call, not
   yours.
5. **Leave a trace.** Tell the Manager what you changed and why — briefly. Your
   improvements should be visible and reviewable (`../TEAM.md` ground rules).

## Guardrails

- **Craft only.** Methodology is yours to edit; mandate, scope, and tools are
  gated.
- **Evidence-based.** Improve from what really happened, not invented lessons.
- **Concise & occasional.** A sharper skill, not a longer one. Per card-close,
  but only when there's a real lesson — skip the no-op edit.
