# Refine the team charter (on demand)

`TEAM.md` is the team's shared brain — every member reads it. As the team learns
and grows, keep it sharp. This is the team-level counterpart to a member's
`self-improve`, and you (the Manager) own it.

## When to use

- **Current goals & priorities** changed — this is the **living** §1 section;
  update it often as priorities move (operational, unless it changes scope).
- The **purpose / north star**, **owner profile**, or **business context** needs
  to shift or sharpen.
- The **roster** changed (you added/removed a member) and the directory or "ladder
  up" lines need updating.
- A **lesson** from real work is worth codifying as a norm or ground rule.
- A section has gone **stale** or vague.

## Classify the change — this decides who approves

- **Strategic** — the north star, the team's scope, what we're for, or a member's
  mandate. → **Propose to the owner, get approval, then write.** (Same gate as
  `init-team` and `add-member`.)
- **Operational** — clarifying a ground rule, updating the directory after a
  roster change, codifying a learned norm, tightening wording. → **You can edit**,
  but **leave a trace**: tell the owner/team what you changed and why.

## Steps

1. Identify the stale/missing piece and which section of `../TEAM.md` it's in.
2. Classify it (strategic vs operational) and get owner approval if strategic.
3. Edit that section — **sharpen, don't bloat.** Cut what's stale; keep it tight.
4. Announce material changes to the team so everyone re-aligns (the whole team
   shares this one charter, so a change affects all — be deliberate).

## Guardrails

- **Strategic = owner-approval; operational = you + a trace.** Never quietly
  change purpose, scope, or a member's mandate.
- **One charter, shared by all** — a careless edit misaligns the whole team. Edit
  with care, keep it concise, and announce.
